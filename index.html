Music Mover Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

Music Mover helps you transfer music libraries and playlists between Spotify and Apple Music. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

No Music Mover Account

Music Mover does not require you to create a Music Mover account. We do not operate a user account system for the app.

No Music Mover Remote Database

Music Mover does not use a Music Mover-operated remote database to store your music library, playlists, transfer history, or account profile. App state needed for transfers is handled on your device.

Spotify

If you connect Spotify, Music Mover uses Spotify authorization to access the Spotify account information needed to read playlists, tracks, and related music metadata for transfers. Spotify access tokens may be stored locally on your device so you can stay connected. Spotify data is used to perform the transfer features you request.

Apple Music

If you connect Apple Music, Music Mover uses Apple Music and MusicKit authorization to access Apple Music catalog and library functionality needed to search for tracks and create or update transferred playlists. Apple Music access is managed through Apple Music authorization on your device.

Local Storage

Music Mover may store connection tokens, preferences, and temporary transfer state locally on your device, including secure device storage such as Keychain where appropriate.

Advertising

Music Mover uses Google AdMob to show ads during free transfers. Google AdMob and related Google services may collect or receive device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate location, diagnostics, and other information as described in Google's policies. Music Mover uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required to collect or respect advertising consent choices.

Third-Party Services

Your use of Spotify, Apple Music, and Google AdMob is also subject to those companies' policies:

  • Spotify Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policy

Children

Music Mover is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
